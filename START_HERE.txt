DIBYANSHI PODDAR PORTFOLIO - how to use this folder

1. Keep everything in this folder TOGETHER. index.html loads its pictures from the files next to it
   (dashboard-1.webp ... dashboard-5.webp and the assets folder). If index.html is moved on its own,
   the pictures will not appear.

2. To preview on your computer: double-click index.html.

3. To publish on GitHub (github.com/dibyanshi29/dibyanshi29.github.io):
   a. Open the repository and click Add file > Upload files.
   b. Drag in EVERYTHING inside this folder: index.html, the dashboard-*.webp files,
      the PDF and the "assets" folder.
   c. Click Commit changes, wait 1 to 2 minutes, then press Ctrl + Shift + R on your site.

4. Optional pictures: add logo-gocomet.png, logo-herofincorp.png and logo-pfizer.png to the assets
   folder if you want company logos in the Experience section. The page works without them.
